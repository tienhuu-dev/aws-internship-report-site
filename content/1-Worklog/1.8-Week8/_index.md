### Week 8 Objectives:

- Understand core AI, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI concepts.
- Learn how to use managed Generative AI models with **Amazon Bedrock**.
- Understand the Machine Learning lifecycle with **Amazon SageMaker AI**.
- Explore managed AI services for image, document, and text analysis.
- Compare AWS AI/ML services and select suitable services for different use cases.
