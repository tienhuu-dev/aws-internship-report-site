### Strengths

* Strong professional knowledge and technical skills in AWS and cloud infrastructure.
* Proactively researches official documentation, community resources, and AI tools to acquire new knowledge.
* Adapts quickly to different AWS services and applies them to practical infrastructure tasks.
* Willingly receives feedback and continuously improves technical and professional capabilities.

### Needs Improvement

* Improve discipline and maintain more consistent compliance with schedules, rules, and working procedures.
* Strengthen systematic problem-solving skills, especially when analyzing technical issues and selecting appropriate solutions.
* Improve communication skills in daily interactions, technical discussions, and work presentations.
* Continue developing the ability to explain technical decisions clearly and concisely.

### Lessons Learned

During the internship, I gained three important areas of knowledge and experience:

* **Networking:** Understanding the role of VPC, subnets, route tables, Internet Gateway, NAT, Security Groups, and VPC endpoints in cloud infrastructure.
* **Real-world AWS deployment services:** Learning how to combine AWS services to design, deploy, test, and review a practical cloud system.
* **Cloud computing trends:** Developing a broader understanding of the rapidly evolving Cloud era and the importance of continuously updating technical knowledge.

I also faced challenges in managing AWS costs and avoiding unnecessary credit usage. In addition, finding suitable documentation was sometimes difficult because of language barriers and outdated materials. To address these issues, I searched for practical use cases shared by the community, used AI tools to support documentation research, watched hands-on lab videos on YouTube, and participated in FCAJ community events.

The internship helped me become more proactive in researching emerging Cloud trends and understanding how they may be applied in future projects.

### Development Orientation

My career goal is to become a **DevOps Engineer**. To achieve this goal, I plan to continue strengthening my AWS and cloud infrastructure knowledge while improving my communication, teamwork, and time management skills. I also aim to develop practical experience in automation, CI/CD, infrastructure as code, monitoring, and cloud security.

### Conclusion

Overall, the internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited** provided valuable opportunities to strengthen my professional knowledge, improve my working attitude, and apply AWS concepts to a practical project. The experience helped me better understand the requirements of a professional environment and reinforced my career orientation toward becoming a **DevOps Engineer**.
